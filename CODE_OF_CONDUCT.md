# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Najm a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, constructive feedback, respect for differing viewpoints, responsibility for mistakes, and focus on what is best for the community. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, sexualized attention, insults, threats, publishing private information, or other conduct inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers clarify and enforce these standards and may remove contributions or participants for harmful behavior. Report conduct concerns privately to the project owner through their GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed promptly and confidentially; retaliation is prohibited.
